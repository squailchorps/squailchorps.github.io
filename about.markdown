## Education
Gregor graduated from Texas Tech University in 2018 with a Bachelor's of Fine Arts in Techncial Theater Design
with an emphasis in Set Desgin and Technical Direction. There I learned how to design from start to finish a set for a theatrical 
production, and took it further by learning how to draft that design and build it on stage. I learned drafting, 
desgin principles, carpentry, communication skills, and finally, how to teach myself. 

I then went later to get my Bachelors of Science in Computer Science, where I currently am and learning. I am about half way through 
my program at Colorado University of Boulder and am making steady progress.

## Career

From 2014-2018, I was a scenic carpenter and part time technician for the Texas Tech Theater Department. 

From 06/2018 to 10/2018, I became an assistant technical director for a small company called Communilux Productions. 
I ended up needing to leave so early due to getting an injury and not being able to work for a month. 

From 02/2019 to 11/2022 I was a teacher for Dallas ISD and Plano ISD as a Technical Theater Teacher and Drawing Teacher.
This was a point I hold dearly in my life for my time being able to give students the ability to build, paint, and Design
anything they wish. I gave students confidence in themselves, and I hope they still carry that to this day. 

From 11/2022 to present, I am a Root Cause Analysis Specialist and Management Consultant. I work with a small 
company known as Galaxiez, where I was the first hire for this company. My main work is performing RCA's over
safety and quality incidents within the steel industry to help improve both areas to be more efficient and 
safe. 

Finally, just recently I took a second job with The Colorado University of Boulder's ECEE department as a procurement specialist, 
helping order and manage all kinds of items for the ECEE department. I specialize in the ordering process, reconciliation, and 
funds management within the ECEE department. So far, for the month I have been here, it has been a wonderful learning experience
into a new area!
